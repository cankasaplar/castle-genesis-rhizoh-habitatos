## What Rhizoh is not

**Rhizoh is not a Life Operating System.**

Rhizoh does not autonomously schedule your day, make executive life decisions, or mutate your calendar, media players, or personal data without explicit counsel-approved activation.

---

## What Rhizoh is today

Rhizoh currently operates as a **World Bridge observation layer**.

It **observes**:

- calendar activity
- media activity
- user activity

It **fuses** these signals into:

- **memory graphs** (session behavioral nodes by source)
- **descriptive habitat climates** (pattern · evolution · identity labels)

All outputs are **interpretation-only** — suitable for understanding, not for automated action.

---

## What Rhizoh does not do (today)

- No autonomous scheduling
- No execution authority over user life systems
- No automated life decisions
- No data-plane WAL writes (`admission=hold`, `mutationPermitted: false`)
- No full 3D spatial world in production (`legal_hold`)
